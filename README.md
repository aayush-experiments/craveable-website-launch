# Craveable Website Launch

Build the first version of the official website for my snack brand Let It Crave.
I have attached two assets:
1. Official Let It Crave logo
Use this exact logo asset. Do not recreate, redraw, retype, modify, or generate a replacement.
2. Hazelnut Chocolate Laddoos packaging design
Treat this as the main visual reference for the website. Study its colors, typography, textures, illustrations, food styling, shapes and overall personality.
Brand: Let It Crave
Tagline: Goodness You Crave, Naturally.
Let It Crave is a modern Indian better-for-you snack brand. Our first product line is date-sweetened nut laddoos.
The website should feel like a natural digital extension of the packaging: warm, craveable, playful, modern and premium but accessible.
Use the packaging's visual language:
- Deep signature red
- Warm cream/off-white
- Dark chocolate/brown
- Beige/peach accents
- Bold playful typography
- Handwritten accents
- Brush-stroke textures
- Organic/rounded shapes
- Ingredient illustrations
- Strong, appetizing food imagery
Do NOT simply turn the packaging into a webpage. Create a spacious, modern D2C/FMCG website from this identity.
Avoid a generic AI/SaaS appearance: no glassmorphism, corporate cards, excessive gradients, random blobs, neon colors or generic startup-style sections.
The food must be the hero of the website.
Build a responsive one-page landing page with these sections:
Hero
Large, visually striking food-focused hero.
Headline: Cravings never tasted this good.
Tagline: Goodness You Crave, Naturally.
Supporting text: Date-sweetened laddoos packed with nuts, flavour and a whole lot of craving.
CTA: Meet the Cravings
Our Cravings
Heading: Pick your craving.
Subheading: Four flavours. One difficult decision.
Show four products:
- Hazelnut Chocolate — rich cacao, dates, almonds and hazelnuts
- Orange Chocolate — chocolate with a bright citrus twist
- Coffee Almond — nutty, rich and coffee-forward
- Vanilla Chocolate — smooth vanilla, chocolate and nuts
Do not add prices or e-commerce yet.
Why Let It Crave
Heading: Made for cravings. Not compromises.
Highlight:
- Sweetened with Dates
- Real Nuts
- Thoughtfully Made
- Flavour First
Ingredients
Heading: You should know what's in your snack.
Visually feature ingredients such as dates, almonds, hazelnuts, cashews, cacao, coconut, coffee and chia seeds.
Our Story
Heading: It started with a craving.
Copy:
“We weren't looking to create another ‘healthy snack.’ We wanted something we'd genuinely reach for when the craving hit — something delicious, satisfying and made from ingredients we actually understood. That's how Let It Crave started.”
Coming Soon
Heading: Something craveable is coming.
Copy: We're getting our first batches ready. Be among the first to try Let It Crave.
Add an early-access form for name + email/phone.
CTA: I Want First Bite
Leave configurable placeholders for Instagram and WhatsApp.
Footer

Use subtle animations and interactions, but don't overdo them.
